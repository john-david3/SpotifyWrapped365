# Spotify Wrapped 365
App that lets you see your Spotify Data from any time period

## How to get your data
- Go to your spotify account on your browser [https://www.spotify.com/account/overview/](https://www.spotify.com/account/overview/)
- Under "Security and Privacy", click "Account Privacy"
- Scroll down to the bottom of the page
- Select one of these options:
  - Account data (You're data for the last year)
  - Extended streaming history (recommended, all time)
- Your data will be sent to you by email (this could take up to 1 month)
- Once you receive your data in JSON format, put the files into the root directory of this repository
